    How to explore

    Farm simulation guide

    Travel: use Overview to orbit, Walk farm for third-person travel, or teleport directly to the well and control room. On desktop, walk with WASD or arrow keys and drag to look. On touch screens, use the directional pad.

    Remote control: near the router the phone uses Wi-Fi; away from it the phone switches to 5G. Both paths represent remote control through the connected Tritonis controller.

    Water feedback: when the pump starts, flow reaches the control-room sensor after a short transport delay. When the pump stops, current returns to zero and the sensor returns to no-flow.

    Faults: with an empty well, the controller allows a 10-second no-flow proving period before latching a dry-run stop. A simulated 32 A high load trips immediately. Reset the fault before restarting.
