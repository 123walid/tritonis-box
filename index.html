      Design basis

      What this prototype shows

      This revised concept uses a 380/400 V AC line-to-line contactor coil: L1 passes through SSR1 to A1 and L2 through SSR2 to A2. Both SSR bodies and their DC control terminals remain inside the closed enclosure; only covered upper AC terminals are accessible. The bottom-facing sensor inputs are a blue-cabled SCT-013-030 current clamp and a yellow-cabled XKC-Y25-NPN water sensor with a flat four-pin plug.

      Production review required

      Validate SSR leakage against coil drop-out, AC coil suppression, protection coordination, thermal performance, EMC, earthing, enclosure ingress and local electrical regulations. The simulation does not certify safety or pump compatibility.
